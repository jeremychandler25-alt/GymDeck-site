♠ GymDeck Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

GymDeck is a deck-of-cards workout app. The short version: your workouts stay on your iPhone. GymDeck has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send your data to us or anyone else.

What GymDeck stores

To work, the app saves these things in a database on your device:

  • Your settings (units, rest time, time cap, jokers on or off)
  • The equipment you own and its weights
  • Your workouts: the cards dealt, reps, weights, time, how hard each set felt, and personal bests

This data never leaves your phone through GymDeck. It's included in your normal iPhone backups (iCloud or computer) the way Apple handles app data.

Apple Health

If you turn it on, GymDeck saves each finished workout to Apple Health as strength training, with its start time, end time and length. GymDeck does not read any Health data. Health information is never used for advertising or shared with anyone. You can switch this off at any time in the Health app or in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → GymDeck.

Sharing

If you tap Share, GymDeck makes a picture of your workout summary and opens the standard iPhone share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose where it goes.

What GymDeck does not do

  • No sign-up, no account, no email collection
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting services
  • No tracking across other apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of data

Deleting your data

You can delete single workouts or clear your history in the app. Deleting GymDeck removes everything it stored on your phone. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Children

GymDeck doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it doesn't collect personal information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
